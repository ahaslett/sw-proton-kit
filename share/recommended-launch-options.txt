Summoners War (Steam 2426960) — recommended Steam launch options
================================================================

Preferred (this package's wrapper):
  "$HOME/.local/bin/sw-compat-wrapper" %command%

ProtonDB consensus without the wrapper:
  %command%

Compatibility tool:
  Proton 9.0-4
  Do not use GE-Proton or Proton Experimental for the login window
  (ProtonDB: those builds hide the Hive/Google login UI).

Also in Steam Properties:
  Disable "Enable the Steam Overlay while in-game"

Optional profiles (wrapper):
  SW_PROTON_PROFILE=diag "$HOME/.local/bin/sw-compat-wrapper" %command%
  SW_PROTON_PROFILE=freeze-mitigation "$HOME/.local/bin/sw-compat-wrapper" %command%

Never:
  WINEDLLOVERRIDES that name GameMon, GameGuard, or npgg*
  Launch options that replace GameGuard binaries
